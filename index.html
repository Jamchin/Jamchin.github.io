PORTRAIT / COMING SOON

NICE TO MEET YOU.

Im James,

I’m currently a Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science student at Northeastern University. I'm incredibly interested in robotics and mechatronics. I enjoy connecting mechanical design, electronics, and software to build things that move and interact with the world. Through personal projects and Northeastern’s Rover team, I’m developing hands-on experience in CAD, prototyping, and programming. My favorite part of engineering is figuring out why something isn't working and bringing the project back to life. A perfect afternoon for me is settling in after a shower on a rainy day, with SolidWorks open and a bowl of frozen blueberries beside me. Outside of engineering, I love drawing and rock climbing, currently working on a V3 @ CRG Fenway.
